PRE-BUILD DECLARATION — Assignment #6, GER Pipeline
Chad Walker · capstone game: Junkstronaut

01. Content type generated manually, inconsistently, or not at all

Debris flavour text. The game spawns 30 junk types from a catalogue holding mass,
altitude, size class and a fragile flag — and nothing a player ever reads. Written by
hand, thirty at a time, they drift.

02. The GDD rule every piece must satisfy

The fiction must match the mechanics. Cargo value and survivability are the same
variable, so a piece's words must agree with its numbers: fragile in the catalogue reads
fragile, the top of the altitude band never reads low, the heaviest piece never reads
light.

03. What a failure looks like, concretely

The player tethers the 3,600 kg Apollo service module at 277,000 m — the piece the
finale is built around — and the hold reads "a light scrap of hull plating, easy haul".
Fluent, and it teaches the opposite of the decision the finale is about.
